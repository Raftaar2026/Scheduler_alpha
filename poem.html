The Goldman

A poem by Rajat Sharma

Coming here, before the first week,
while walking along a creek —
away from the honk of cars,
was enjoying the greeneries of grass.

Across the creek, on my left, saw a creature…
Cleared my specs — it was a tiger!
Sun already had set, when our eyes met.

It growled, challenging me for a fight.
Given the brave man I was,
I gathered my strength, turned right —
and ran with all my might!

After a few hundred metres,
and some quivers…
I fell — into what looked like a well.

Inside the well, in dark night,
under the moonlight,
something was glittering bright…
Coins! Coins of gold!
Gold, with heaps of so many bags,
that I felt like the Goldman of Goldman Sachs.

I dropped my plan of IIM;
thought — will open a bank named The Goldway,
and hire MBAs!

My ringtone went up…
Rolling my eyes — I woke up!

“Good morning, Sir,” said someone on the phone,
“I am from SBI — come sign your loan.”

I am running out of time,
and words that can rhyme…
So, my dear folks —
that’s where I close.
